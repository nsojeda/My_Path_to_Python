# Module Arithmetic
---

Modules shows what is left after division

Best example I've seen (can't remember where it was)

150 cupcake are made in 1 day
12 cupcake go in 1 box

How many cupcake are left at the end of the day?
```python
print(150 % 12)
```

150 % 12 = 6
150 / 12=12.5
.5 \* 12= 6

12 boxes of 12 cupcakes is 144
so, 6 cupcakes are left


```python
print(2 % 2) # =0

print(3 % 2) # =1

print(4 % 2) # =0
```
