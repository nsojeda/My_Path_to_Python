# Order of Operations
---

Python follow the standard rules of operations

 **Order of operations** 
 is the standard set of rules for evaluating mathematical expressions so everyone gets the same answer.
 
 The Rules (PEMDAS / BODMAS)
 
 1. **P**arentheses / **B**rackets — solve innermost first
 2. **E**xponents / **O**rders — powers and roots
 3. **M**ultiplication and **D**ivision — left to right
 4. **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction — left to right
 
 > Multiplication does not come before division; they have equal priority and are done in the order they appear from left to right. Same with addition and subtraction.
 

> [!Example]
>  3 + 6 × (5 + 4) ÷ 3² − 1
> parentheses = 3 + 6 × 9 ÷ 3² − 1   
> exponent = 3 + 6 × 9 ÷ 9 − 1       
> multiplication (left to right) = 3 + 54 ÷ 9 − 1          
> division = 3 + 6 − 1  
> addition = 9 − 1                   
> subtraction = 8
